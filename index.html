What Makes a Zulfa Pakistani 3-Piece Suit Special?

Rooted in the historic textile tradition of Pakistan, every suit is an intricate harmony of fine fabrics, unstitched freedom, and artisanal finishing.

Standard 3-Piece Ensemble Breakdown
KAMEEZ / SHIRT Full-length pure crinkle chiffon or supima lawn with hand-embroidered neckline, front jaal & sleeves.
DUPATTA 2.5 to 2.8 meters of hand-cut scallop organza, pure silk, or Banarasi zari with finished borders.
TROUSER / SHALWAR Dyed raw silk or cambric cotton with embroidered hem patti for tailored cigarette pants or classic shalwar.
MATCHING HIJAB Optional color-dyed Turkish satin, modal, or georgette hijab designed to complement the dupatta.
